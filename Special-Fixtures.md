To ease testing, here are some fixtures with special properties:

PAN/TILT:
* 8 bit Pan & 8 bit Tilt: Futurelight/DJScan250
* 16 bit & 16 bit Tilt: Futurelight/MH-440
* Pan and no Tilt: Blizzard Lighting/Snowball; Futurelight/CY-200
* Multiple heads with (separate) Pan/Tilt: American DJ/Nucleus LED, American DJ/Event Bar LED, Showtec XS-6 (no fixture def yet)
* Multiple heads with common Tilt: American DJ/Sweeper Beam Quad LED

RGB/Dimmer:

* no master dimmer + 10 heads { dimmer }: Showtec/Sunstrip Active
* master dimmer + 4 heads { dimmer }: American DJ/Event Bar LED
* 64 heads { RGB } no master dimmer: American DJ/Revo 4 - 256-CH
* 64 heads { RGBW } no master dimmer: American DJ/Hyper Gem LED
* 4 heads { Dimmer + RGB } no master dimmer: American DJ/Quad Scan LED
* Master dimmer + 4 heads { RGBW (no per-head dimmer) }: American DJ/Event Bar Q4 (37 channel mode)
* RGB single head + Dimmer: American DJ/64B LED PRO
* RGBA single head + Dimmer: American DJ/COB Cannon Wash Pearl
* RGBW single head + Dimmer: American DJ/Dekker LED
* RGBAW single head + Dimmer: Blizzard Lighting/Puck RGBAW
* RGBAW-UV single head + Dimmer: American DJ/12P Hex Pearl

This is place to collect ideas about features for rock shows, before we can move it to http://qlcplus.org/usage_rock.html

- Fixtures, universes and channels groups

- PAR control
  - it's easy to simulate simple controllers with scenes, faders, chasers, and flash buttons
  - intensity control with Virtual Console sliders

- Moving heads
  - EFX
  - simple fanning
  - movement control with Virtual Console XY Pad
  - quick access to gobos in a completely visual way thanks to the Click & Go technology

- Pixel mapping
  - for LED bars, for arbitrary fixtures

- Flexible control - MIDI controllers, real DMX controllers, keyboard, mouse, touch.

- Audio driven light
  - control lights intensity with audio input volume or audio spectrum bands
